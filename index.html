02
Support

Let's stay in touch.

For studio enquiries and future application support, reach us by email. We read everything, and reply as a small team can.

  • For an app issue, include the app name and version.
  • Tell us your Android version and device.
  • Add a short description of what you expected and what happened.

Please do not email passwords, payment card details, identity documents, or verification codes. We will never ask for them.

Email the studio
evervoya.studio@gmail.com

We are a small team, so please allow a little time for a reply.

03
Support

Let's stay in touch.

For studio enquiries and future application support, reach us by email. We read everything, and reply as a small team can.

  • For an app issue, include the app name and version.
  • Tell us your Android version and device.
  • Add a short description of what you expected and what happened.

Please do not email passwords, payment card details, identity documents, or verification codes. We will never ask for them.

Email the studio
evervoya.studio@gmail.com

We are a small team, so please allow a little time for a reply.

Privacy information

Specific to each app.

Each app has its own privacy policy, published here before it is released. It describes, feature by feature, what stays on the device, what leaves it and why, the third-party SDKs involved (including advertising where applicable), permissions, retention and deletion.

Evervoya ProductLog — privacy policy (effective 30 October 2026). Policies for Genivo, Optivo and Sentivo follow when those apps reach testing.

Every Evervoya app also carries a Disclaimer & limitation of liability and Terms of Service inside the app (Settings → Legal), agreed on first run.

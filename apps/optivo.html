Optivo

Your site, your AI key, your next five fixes.

AndroidIn development

Most small sites never get an SEO review because the tools cost more than the site earns. Optivo runs the checks from your phone, hands the findings to the AI provider you already use, with your own API key, and comes back with actions a human can do this afternoon: fix this title, speed up that image, write these two paragraphs.

Without it

  • SEO suites want a monthly subscription that costs more than your hosting.
  • Reports arrive as sixty red items with no idea which three matter.
  • "Improve Core Web Vitals" is not an instruction anyone can act on at a warung table.
  • You already pay for an AI assistant, but it has never seen your actual pages.
  • Agencies quote by the month; you needed one honest look.

With Optivo

  • Paste a URL. Optivo fetches the page and the speed data and lays out what it found.
  • Your own key, your own model, your own cost: the AI explains the findings and ranks the fixes.
  • Every suggestion is written for a person, not a crawler: what to change, where, and why it matters.
  • Run it again after the fix and watch the list get shorter.
  • Nothing of yours is stored on an Evervoya server; the key stays on your device.

"It felt like having a patient consultant who reads my page, points at three things, and does not send an invoice."

Strengths

  • Bring your own AIUse the provider and model you trust and pay for; switch any time. Optivo adds no AI fee.
  • Real page dataChecks run on the live page and on public speed metrics, not on guesses.
  • Prioritised, plain-language actionsA short list, ordered by impact, each with the exact change to make.
  • Built for phonesReview a page from anywhere; share the action list with whoever edits the site.
  • Private by designKeys and results stay on your device; no account with Evervoya.
  • Grows with your sitesKeep several sites and compare runs over time.

Honest limits

  • AI output can be wrong, incomplete or dated. Treat suggestions as informed opinion and verify before publishing.
  • No guarantee of rankings or traffic. Search engines decide; Optivo helps you do the work well.
  • Your provider bills you for usage. Optivo shows what it sends so costs stay predictable, but the key and its terms are yours.

Who it is for

Small business owners with a website and no SEO budget, freelancers who manage a few client sites, bloggers and shop owners who want a second pair of eyes before every publish.

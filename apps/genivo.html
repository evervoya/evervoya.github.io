Genivo

Your turn. Their turn. Who really knows more?

AndroidIn development

Genivo is a one-on-one trivia duel that fits around your day. Open a duel with a friend, pick a category, answer your round, and get on with life. When they answer theirs, you get a nudge. No lobbies, no countdown to join, no excuse not to settle who knows football, history or film better.

Without it

  • Quiz apps demand that both of you are free at the same minute, so the duel never happens.
  • The questions are the same tired set you saw last month; the challenge fades in a week.
  • "Random category" means ten questions about things nobody at the table cares about.
  • Losing ends the conversation. There is no way to say "again, my pick".
  • Rankings mix everyone into one global list where you will never appear.

With Genivo

  • Play when you can. Each round is yours to answer whenever; the duel waits.
  • A bank of more than four thousand questions in eight main categories, kept fresh without an app update.
  • The round opener picks the category from a small offer; Sports is always on the table.
  • Lost? Ask for a rematch and suggest the category. Win a category duel against a real opponent and you become its champion.
  • No internet? Duel the person next to you over a direct device-to-device link, or practise alone.

"The duel is still open at breakfast. You answer three questions with your coffee, and that is the whole game until tonight."

Strengths

  • Asynchronous duelsTurn-based rounds, so a match survives meetings, commutes and time zones.
  • Eight main categoriesSports, Science & Nature, Technology & Business, Geography & Cultures, History & People, Movies & Music, Games & Pop Culture, Arts, Myths & Mysteries.
  • Three ways to playOnline with anyone, nearby over a direct connection with no internet, or practice by yourself.
  • Rematch with a twistThe loser proposes; the winner picks the category or lets fate decide.
  • Category championsRankings per category, not just one global pile, plus an overall rating that moves with every duel.
  • Six languagesQuestions and interface, so a duel across borders is fair.
  • Fresh questionsThe bank updates server-side; new facts arrive without waiting for a new version.
  • Light monetisationOne banner outside the duel screen, never during play, and a one-time purchase removes it.

Honest limits

  • Online duels need a Google account and a connection. Nearby and practice modes work without either.
  • Questions can be wrong or go out of date. They are for fun and general knowledge, not a reference; report one and it gets fixed.
  • Other players are other people. Names and behaviour are theirs; rankings and badges have no monetary value.

Who it is for

Friends who argue about football, families spread across cities, colleagues who need something better than a group chat for bragging rights. Anyone who likes to be right and can wait until tonight to prove it.

Sentivo

Two hundred notifications a day. How did they leave you?

Android 8.0+In developmentOn-device only

Your phone already knows which chats light up your evening and which ones tighten your shoulders. Sentivo reads the notifications that are already arriving, from the apps you choose, and turns them into a quiet daily picture: what lifted you, what drained you, where the noise came from. All of it is calculated on the device. Nothing leaves your phone.

Without it

  • A heavy day is a blur; you only know you feel worse and the phone was involved.
  • The group that stresses you most is also the loudest, so it never gets muted.
  • Wellbeing apps ask you to journal every evening, and you stop on day four.
  • The apps that promise insight want your messages uploaded to someone's server.
  • Screen-time numbers tell you how long, never how it felt.

With Sentivo

  • Nothing to type. The signal is in what already arrives; Sentivo listens so you do not have to.
  • One calm rating circle for the day, and the sources behind it, so you can see what moved it.
  • Spot the pattern: the channel that spikes at 11 pm, the week that got steadily heavier.
  • Decide with evidence: mute, leave, or simply know that today was a good one.
  • No internet permission at all. Your messages are never uploaded, because the app cannot.

"I did not need an app to tell me the family group was exhausting. I needed to see it on a Tuesday, in a number, before I replied."

Strengths

  • Zero effortNo diary, no check-in prompts. The picture builds from notifications you already receive.
  • Truly privateProcessing is on the device and the app requests no internet permission. There is nothing to leak.
  • You choose the sourcesThirteen supported messaging and social apps; pick which ones count.
  • Daily rating circleOne honest figure per day with the breakdown behind it, and the trend over weeks.
  • Evidence for small decisionsMute, leave, reply later: the choices that add up, backed by your own data.
  • Plain about its limitsSentivo says what it is and is not, in the app, on first run and every day after.

Honest limits

  • Not a medical, psychological or emergency service. Ratings are automated estimates from text and timing; they can be wrong. If you or someone else may be in danger, contact your local emergency number or a professional.
  • Needs notification access to work at all; Android asks you to grant it, and you can revoke it any time.
  • It sees what notifications show. Muted chats and apps that hide content give it less to work with.

Who it is for

People who feel their phone more than they measure it: parents in six group chats, anyone whose work lives in messaging apps, anyone curious whether the feed is helping.

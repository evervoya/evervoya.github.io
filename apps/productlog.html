Evervoya ProductLog

The day something breaks, you already have the receipt.

Android 7.0+Closed testingGoogle Play soon

Washing machine, laptop, air conditioner, the kids' tablet. Every one came with a receipt, a warranty card and a service hotline, and every one of those is now in a drawer, a chat thread or the bin. ProductLog is the one place where all of it lives, on your own phone, ready the moment you need it.

Without it

  • The fridge dies in month eleven of a twelve-month warranty, and the receipt is nowhere.
  • You pay for a repair that the store warranty would have covered, because nobody remembered there was one.
  • The service centre asks for the serial number and the purchase date; you are on your knees behind the appliance with a torch.
  • The air-conditioner filter was due for cleaning three months ago. Nobody noticed until the bill did.
  • A claim goes in, then silence. Which ticket? Which date? Who did you talk to?

With ProductLog

  • Photograph the receipt once; the app reads the store, date and price and fills the form for you to confirm.
  • Home shows what is expiring soon before it does, with reminders at 30, 14 and 7 days, or whatever you choose.
  • Tap Call on the claim contact. The warranty card? The app knows which drawer you put it in.
  • Periodic care gets its own schedule and its own gentle nudge.
  • Export a claim pack: history, documents and proof in one zip, ready to hand to the service centre or insurer.

"It is the feeling of opening a drawer and finding exactly the paper you need, every time, except the drawer is your phone."

Strengths

  • Everything about a product in one cardBrand, serial, store and address, price, purchase date, claim contact and phone, where the warranty card is kept, notes.
  • Several protections per productManufacturer, store and extended warranties, each with its own dates, coverage, exclusions and contact. The app watches the one that ends last.
  • Scan instead of typeReceipts and warranty cards are read on the device; you review the suggestions before anything is saved.
  • Claims you can followDraft, submitted, waiting, in progress, done or rejected, with ticket number, service centre, follow-up date and outcome. Home reminds you to chase.
  • Reminders that fit your lifePick the days before a warranty ends or care is due. Each reminder fires once and opens the product when tapped.
  • Care history and insightsEvery repair, cleaning and part replacement is logged. Risk insights show which brands and products keep giving you trouble.
  • Repair or replace?An honest estimate from the repair quote, the price of a new unit and the product's age, so you stop paying twice.
  • Documents and proof, kept privateReceipts, manuals and warranty cards as PDF or images, stored only on your phone. Full ZIP backups to a folder you choose.
  • Works offline, no account neededNo Evervoya server. Google sign-in is optional and only identifies you.
  • English and Bahasa IndonesiaSwitch in Settings without restarting.

Honest limits

  • Scanning reads printed text only. Handwritten cards still need typing; you can attach the photo as a document instead.
  • Your phone is the home of your data. There is no cloud sync yet; back up with the ZIP export before changing phones.
  • The free version shows one small banner at the bottom of the product list. A one-time purchase removes it for good.
  • Android only, 7.0 and up.

Who it is for

Anyone who has ever said "I know we still have the warranty somewhere." Households with more than a handful of appliances, people who rent out rooms or flats with furnished equipment, small offices that want one honest record of what they bought and when.

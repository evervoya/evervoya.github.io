Evervoya ProductLog

Every product you own. Receipts, warranties and care in one place.

Android 7.0+Closed testingGoogle Play soon

ProductLog is home appliance asset management for one household: what you own, what is still under warranty, what needs care, and the proof you will need when something breaks.

What it does

Products and proof

Name, brand, serial, store, price, purchase date, where the warranty card is kept, who to call for a claim. Attach receipts, manuals and warranty cards (PDF or images).

Warranties and claims

Several protections per product (manufacturer, store, extended) with their own dates, coverage and contacts. Track a claim from draft to done with follow-up reminders.

Scan, don't type

Receipt and warranty-card scanning runs on the device and suggests the fields; you review before saving. Handwritten cards can be attached as documents instead.

Care and reminders

Service history, periodic care schedules, and reminders you tune yourself (days before a warranty ends or care is due). A tapped reminder opens the product.

Insights and advice

Which brands and products give warranty trouble, from your own records. A repair-or-replace estimate from the quote, the price of a new unit and the product's age.

Claim pack and backup

Export a product's history, documents and a consistency-checked care ledger as one zip for a claim. Full backups (ZIP) or records only (JSON), to a folder you choose.

Built to stay on your phone

Products, documents and history live on the device. There is no Evervoya server and no account is required. Google sign-in is optional and only identifies you; it does not upload your products. The free version shows one small banner on the product list and nothing else; a one-time purchase removes it. Everything else is in the privacy policy.

Availability

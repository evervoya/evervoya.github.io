Privacy policy

Evervoya Optivo

Effective 13 November 2026 (app version 0.1.5) · Applies to the Android app "Evervoya Optivo" (package com.evervoya.optivo) by Evervoya Studio

Optivo checks the search signals of websites you own or manage. It is local-first: your websites, scans, findings and settings are stored on your phone, and the app works fully without any account. Signing in to Google is optional and identity-only, and an optional Pro feature can sync your workspace — encrypted on your phone — through your own Google Drive. Evervoya Studio runs no server for Optivo and does not receive your workspace, your website list, your API keys or your synced data. Some features send data directly from your phone to third parties (including, with your choices, Google); this policy lists each one.

1. Optional Google sign-in (identity only)

Optivo works fully without signing in. You may optionally sign in with your Google account in Settings → Account using Google's "Sign in with Google". When you do, Optivo receives only your Google profile basics — your name, e-mail address and profile photo — and keeps them on the phone to show who is signed in. Signing in requests no access to your Google data: no Drive-wide access, no Search Console, no Analytics. You can sign out at any time (Settings → Account → Sign out), which removes these details from the phone. Optivo also stores, on the phone only, the version and date of the Disclaimer & Terms you agreed to. (Versions before 0.1.4 had a different, broader Google sign-in with Search Console, Analytics and Drive sync; that was removed in 0.1.4. The sign-in reintroduced in 0.1.5 is identity-only, and the sync below uses only the app's own private Drive folder.)

3. Data that stays on your phone

Websites, scans, findings, fix progress, saved AI action plans and settings are kept in the app's private storage and are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Your Google API key and AI API keys are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. If you sign in, your Google name, e-mail and photo are stored here too, and are removed when you sign out. If you turn on device sync, the key that encrypts your synced data is also kept in this private, Keystore-protected storage. The free version keeps the last 5 scans per website, Pro keeps 100. Google responses are cached for a few hours to save your quota.

Backup & restore writes your workspace to a file at the place you choose with Android's file picker. That export is separate from device sync, uploads nothing on its own, and where the file goes afterwards is up to you.

4. Device sync (Pro, optional)

Optivo Pro can keep your workspace in step across your own devices using your own Google Drive. Evervoya runs no sync server and never holds your data or your passphrase. It works like this:

  • You turn it on in Settings → Device sync and choose a passphrase (8+ characters). The passphrase never leaves your phone and is never sent to Google or to Evervoya.
  • Your workspace is encrypted on the phone with a key protected by your passphrase before anything is uploaded. Only the encrypted result is stored, in the hidden "app data" folder of your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata scope), which only Optivo on your own devices can see. Optivo requests no other access to your Drive and cannot see your other files.
  • Because the data is end-to-end encrypted and the key is yours, Evervoya cannot read your synced data, and neither can anyone else with access to your Drive who does not have your passphrase. If you forget the passphrase, the cloud copy cannot be recovered; your devices keep their local data.
  • To stop syncing on a device, use Settings → Device sync → Turn off. To remove the encrypted copy from your Google Drive, use Settings → Device sync → Delete cloud copy, which deletes Optivo's files from your Drive app folder and turns sync off on that device. You can also revoke Optivo's Drive access at any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps and services.

5. Advertising

The free version shows one banner served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To do this, the SDK may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and device and app information, under Google's advertising policies. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is loaded, and "Ad privacy choices" in Settings lets you change it. No website addresses, findings or other workspace content are passed to AdMob. The measurement library bundled in the Ads SDK is turned off. Optivo Pro shows no ads and does not start the Ads SDK.

6. Subscriptions

Optivo Pro is a Google Play subscription, processed by Google Play Billing under Google Play's terms and privacy policy. The app receives only the subscription state and stores it on the phone. Evervoya does not receive your payment details. Google's Play Billing Library itself sends usage diagnostics to Google; these contain no workspace data.

7. What Optivo does not collect

No IMEI, MAC address, hardware serial, SMS, contacts, call logs or precise location. Optivo adds no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own.

Each is governed by its own terms and privacy policy. You are responsible for any fees your own AI provider charges you.

10. Delete your data

Optivo creates no account on an Evervoya server, so there is no online account to delete. In the app:

  • Settings → Privacy → Delete all local data erases websites, scans, plans, keys, sync keys and settings;
  • Settings → Account → Sign out removes the signed-in Google name, e-mail and photo from the phone;
  • Settings → Device sync → Delete cloud copy removes the encrypted sync files from your Google Drive app folder, and Turn off stops syncing on that device;
  • Settings → PageSpeed and Settings → AI let you remove a key you added;
  • a backup file you exported is yours to delete wherever you saved it.

Uninstalling the app removes all data on the phone. You can remove Optivo's Google access (the optional sign-in and the Drive app-folder sync) in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps and services. Feedback e-mails can be deleted on request to evervoya.studio@gmail.com.

11. Children

Optivo is a tool for website owners and is not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live).

12. Changes and contact

If the app's data handling changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date. Questions and requests: evervoya.studio@gmail.com.

Evervoya Optivo

Effective 12 November 2026 (app version 0.1.4) · Applies to the Android app "Evervoya Optivo" (package com.evervoya.optivo) by Evervoya Studio

Optivo checks the search signals of websites you own or manage. It is local-first: your websites, scans, findings and settings are stored on your phone. There is no sign-in and no account. Evervoya Studio runs no server for Optivo and does not receive your workspace, your website list or your API keys. Some features send data directly from your phone to third parties; this policy lists each one.

1. No sign-in, no account

Optivo never asks you to sign in to Google or any other service, and Evervoya does not request access to any Google API on your behalf. Optivo stores, on the phone only, the version and date of the Disclaimer & Terms you agreed to. Versions before 0.1.4 offered an optional Google sign-in, Search Console, Google Analytics and Drive sync; these were removed in 0.1.4, and updating the app stops them.

3. Data that stays on your phone

Websites, scans, findings, fix progress, saved AI action plans and settings are kept in the app's private storage and are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. Your Google API key and AI API keys are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. The free version keeps the last 5 scans per website, Pro keeps 100. Google responses are cached for a few hours to save your quota.

Backup & restore writes your workspace to a file at the place you choose with Android's file picker. Optivo uploads nothing; where the file goes afterwards is up to you.

4. Advertising

The free version shows one banner served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To do this, the SDK may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and device and app information, under Google's advertising policies. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is loaded, and "Ad privacy choices" in Settings lets you change it. No website addresses, findings or other workspace content are passed to AdMob. The measurement library bundled in the Ads SDK is turned off. Optivo Pro shows no ads and does not start the Ads SDK.

5. Subscriptions

Optivo Pro is a Google Play subscription, processed by Google Play Billing under Google Play's terms and privacy policy. The app receives only the subscription state and stores it on the phone. Evervoya does not receive your payment details. Google's Play Billing Library itself sends usage diagnostics to Google; these contain no workspace data.

6. What Optivo does not collect

No IMEI, MAC address, hardware serial, SMS, contacts, call logs or precise location. Optivo adds no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own.

8. Third-party services

Google PageSpeed Insights API and Chrome UX Report API (with your own key), Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform, Google Play Billing, Google Apps Script (feedback relay), and the AI provider or AI app you choose (for example Google Gemini or OpenAI). Each is governed by its own terms and privacy policy. You are responsible for any fees your own AI provider charges you.

9. Delete your data

Optivo creates no account on an Evervoya server, so there is no online account to delete. In the app:

  • Settings → Privacy → Delete all local data erases websites, scans, plans, keys and settings;
  • Settings → PageSpeed and Settings → AI let you remove a key you added;
  • a backup file you exported is yours to delete wherever you saved it.

Uninstalling the app removes all data on the phone. If you used Google sign-in in a version before 0.1.4, you can remove that old access in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps. Feedback e-mails can be deleted on request to evervoya.studio@gmail.com.

10. Children

Optivo is a tool for website owners and is not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live).

11. Changes and contact

If the app's data handling changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date. Questions and requests: evervoya.studio@gmail.com.

Privacy policy

Evervoya Optivo

Effective 3 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app "Evervoya Optivo" (package com.evervoya.optivo) by Evervoya Studio

Optivo checks the search signals of websites you own or manage. It is local-first: your websites, scans, findings and settings are stored on your phone. Evervoya Studio runs no server for Optivo and does not receive your workspace, your website list, your AI keys or your Google data. Some features send data directly from your phone to third parties; this policy lists each one.

1. Signing in

Optivo asks you to sign in with Google before first use. Google performs the sign-in. Optivo keeps your Google account identifier, name and e-mail address on your phone only, to show who is signed in. Evervoya does not create an account on any server. Optivo also stores, on the phone, the version and date of the Disclaimer & Terms you agreed to.

3. Data that stays on your phone

Websites, scans, findings, fix progress, saved AI action plans and settings are kept in the app's private storage and are excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. AI API keys and the sync key are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore. The free version keeps the last 5 scans per website, Pro keeps 100. Google responses are cached for a few hours to save your quota.

Device sync encrypts your workspace on the phone (AES-256-GCM) with a key protected by your passphrase before anything is uploaded. Evervoya cannot read it. If you forget the passphrase, the cloud copy cannot be recovered; your phones keep their own data.

4. Advertising

The free version shows one banner served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To do this, the SDK may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and device and app information, under Google's advertising policies. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is loaded, and "Ad privacy choices" in Settings lets you change it. No website addresses, findings or other workspace content are passed to AdMob. The measurement library bundled in the Ads SDK is turned off. Optivo Pro shows no ads and does not start the Ads SDK.

5. Subscriptions

Optivo Pro is a Google Play subscription, processed by Google Play Billing under Google Play's terms and privacy policy. The app receives only the subscription state and stores it on the phone. Evervoya does not receive your payment details. Google's Play Billing Library itself sends usage diagnostics to Google; these contain no workspace data.

6. What Optivo does not collect

No IMEI, MAC address, hardware serial, SMS, contacts, call logs or precise location. Optivo adds no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own.

9. Delete your data

Optivo creates no account on an Evervoya server, so there is no online account to delete. In the app:

  • Settings → Account → Sign out forgets your Google account on the phone;
  • Settings → Privacy → Delete all local data erases websites, scans, plans, keys and settings and signs you out;
  • Settings → Google connection → Disconnect revokes Optivo's access at Google;
  • Settings → Device sync → Delete cloud copy removes the encrypted copy from your Google Drive.

Uninstalling the app removes all data on the phone. You can also remove Optivo's access at any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps. Feedback e-mails can be deleted on request to evervoya.studio@gmail.com.

10. Children

Optivo is a tool for website owners and is not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live).

11. Changes and contact

If the app's data handling changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date. Questions and requests: evervoya.studio@gmail.com.

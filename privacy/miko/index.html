Miko the Civet: Flower Trail

Effective 4 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app "Miko the Civet: Flower Trail" (package com.evervoya.mikotrail) by Evervoya Studio

Miko the Civet: Flower Trail is a game made for young children (ages 3–7), to be used with a parent or guardian. It is built to comply with Google Play's Families policy and, where they apply, laws for services directed to children such as COPPA and GDPR-K. This policy explains what the app keeps, the little that leaves the device and why, and the choices a parent has.

1. No account, and everything playable stays on the device

The app has no account and no sign-in. It stores on your device only:

  • settings and language;
  • trail progress, coins, wardrobe items and learning progress;
  • optionally, a child's first name and birthday (month and day only) that a parent may type in Settings to personalise greetings.

None of this is uploaded to us or to anyone else. Spoken prompts are produced by the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device.

2. When the app uses the network

The game itself plays fully offline. The app contacts the internet only for three things: to show the banner advertisement (section 3), to process an optional purchase (section 4), and when a parent sends feedback (section 6). There are no analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.

3. Advertising — one child-directed banner

The free game shows one banner advertisement on the menu screens only — never while your child is playing a trail, and never as a full-screen, interstitial or rewarded ad. It is served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), a Google Play Families-certified ad SDK, configured for child-directed treatment with a maximum ad content rating of G and non-personalised ads.

Because the ads are non-personalised and child-directed, they are chosen by context, not by a profile of your child. To show and frequency-cap the ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK may process your device and app information, coarse (country-level) location derived from your IP address, and an on-device identifier. Your child's data is not used to build an advertising profile or for personalised advertising, and we do not sell it. See Google's advertising policies. Buying any coin pack (section 4) removes the ads permanently.

4. Purchases — optional, behind a parent gate

The app offers optional in-app coin packs, purchased through Google Play Billing under Google Play's terms and privacy policy. Purchases sit behind a parent gate. The app receives only the purchase state and stores it on the device; Evervoya never receives your payment details. Buying any coin pack also removes the banner advertisement for good.

5. No analytics or tracking

The app includes no analytics SDK and no tracking SDK. We do not measure your child's behaviour in the app, and nothing about how your child plays is sent to us.

6. Feedback a parent sends

When a parent uses the Feedback button, the app sends only: the message typed, the app version and language, and — only if the box is ticked — the device model. It goes to the studio's mailbox (evervoya.studio@gmail.com) through a relay (Google Apps Script). No other data is attached. Feedback e-mails are kept only as long as needed to handle them and can be deleted on request.

7. Permissions

PermissionUsed for
InternetShowing the banner ad, processing an optional purchase, and sending feedback.

8. Third-party services

Google Mobile Ads SDK / AdMob (the child-directed banner), Google Play Billing (optional purchases), and Google Apps Script (feedback relay). Each is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy. No other third-party service receives data from the app.

9. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. The only data that leaves the device goes to the Google services named above, and only to serve the non-personalised ad, process a purchase you make, or deliver feedback you send.

10. Parents' choices

Because everything about play stays on the device, a parent can erase it all by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app; any name or birthday entered in Settings is removed with it. The "remove ads" purchase stays with your Google Play account.

11. Children

This app is directed to children and is part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond the on-device items described above, we serve only non-personalised, child-directed ads, and we do not build advertising profiles of children.

12. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date. Questions and requests: evervoya.studio@gmail.com.

The in-app Credits & Legal page (Settings → Credits & Legal) shows the Disclaimer, Terms of Service and licences, which apply together with this policy.

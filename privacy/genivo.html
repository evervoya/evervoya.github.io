Privacy policy

Genivo

Effective 30 October 2026 · Applies to the Android app "Genivo" (package studio.evervoya.triviaduel) by Evervoya Studio

Genivo is a quiz duel game. This policy describes what the app stores, what leaves your phone and why, which third-party services are involved, and how you delete your account and data.

1. Your account and online duels

When you open the app it creates an account without asking for your name or e-mail address. Online duels and the leaderboard run on Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), which stores:

  • a random account identifier;
  • your player name, which the app assigns at random and which you can change with the purchase in section 5;
  • your rating, wins and category wins;
  • your duels: opponent, questions, answers, scores, invitations and dates.

Your player name, rating and duel results are visible to other players. Do not use your real name if you prefer not to be identified.

Counts of how often an answer was chosen, used by the Players help, are stored per question without any link to you.

2. Optional Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, Google's Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication link your account to your Google account identifier and e-mail address. Your Google name and e-mail address are never shown to other players.

3. Data that stays on your phone

Your settings, category statistics from the advisor, composure results from the Surprise me mode, your invitation list and helps used in a round are stored only on your phone. Translating questions into your language runs on the phone with Google ML Kit, after one download of the language model. Android may include app data in the device backup it makes to your Google account; that backup is run and controlled by Android and by you.

4. Advertising

The free version shows a banner served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To do this, the SDK may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and device and app information, under Google's advertising policies. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is loaded. Advertising stops after the one-time "remove ads" purchase.

5. Purchases

The one-time "remove ads" purchase, which also unlocks Pro features, is processed by Google Play Billing under Google Play's terms and privacy policy. The app receives only the purchase state and stores it on the phone. Evervoya does not receive your payment details.

6. Analytics

The app includes Google Analytics for Firebase, which collects app usage events, device information and an app instance identifier so we can understand how the game is used. It is not used to show you ads from Evervoya.

7. Feedback you send

When you send feedback, the app sends your message, the feedback type, the app version and language, and, if you are signed in with Google, your e-mail address (so we can reply) to Evervoya Studio. Device details are included only if you choose. Feedback e-mails are kept as long as needed to handle them.

8. Notifications and background checks

Rematch invitations are shown as local notifications. The app checks for them about every 15 minutes while the phone is online. You can turn notifications off in Android settings.

Each is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy.

11. Delete your account and data

In the app: open Settings, then Delete account and data. This removes:

  • your profile, rating and wins;
  • your Firebase account;
  • open lobbies nobody joined;
  • your name and rating from duels with other players;
  • everything the app keeps on the phone.

An unfinished duel is forfeited first. Your opponent keeps their history of the duel, without your name or rating. The purchase stays with your Google Play account.

Without the app: write to evervoya.studio@gmail.com with the subject "Genivo account deletion" and your player name, and your rating or the date of a recent duel so we can identify the account. We handle the request by hand and confirm by e-mail.

Uninstalling the app removes the data on the phone but not your online account. Feedback e-mails can be deleted on request.

12. Children

Genivo is not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

13. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date. Questions and requests: evervoya.studio@gmail.com.

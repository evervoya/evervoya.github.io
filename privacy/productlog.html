Privacy policy

Evervoya ProductLog

Effective 30 October 2026 · Applies to the Android app "Evervoya ProductLog" (package com.evervoya.warranty) by Evervoya Studio

ProductLog is built to keep your data on your phone. This policy describes, feature by feature, what the app stores, what leaves the device and why, which third-party services are involved, and how you delete your data.

1. Data that stays on your device

Everything you enter or attach is stored only in the app's private storage on your device: products, brands, serial numbers, purchase details, store and claim contacts, warranties and claims, service and care records, documents (receipts, manuals, warranty cards), optional compressed proof images, the care ledger, and your settings. Receipt and warranty-card scanning (text recognition) runs on the device; the original photo is not copied or retained unless you choose to keep a compressed proof image. There is no Evervoya server, and the app does not upload this data.

Backups and claim packs are written only where you choose in the system file picker. They are plain files: anyone who holds the file can read it.

2. Optional Google sign-in

You can use the app fully without an account ("local mode"). If you choose Continue with Google, Google's Credential Manager (Sign in with Google) returns your Google account identifier, display name, e-mail address and profile picture URL. The app stores these on your device as the owner identity and shows them in Settings. The sign-in token is used once to read that identity and is not stored or sent to Evervoya. Signing out removes the stored identity; your products are not affected. Google's own privacy policy applies to the sign-in itself.

3. Feedback you send

When you tap Send in the feedback form, the app sends your message, the feedback type, the app version and language, and, if you are signed in, your Google e-mail address (so we can reply) to Evervoya Studio. Device model and Android version are included only if you tick that box. The message is relayed through a Google Apps Script owned by Evervoya Studio and delivered to our mailbox; Google processes it as our service provider. Serial numbers, documents, products and other records are never included. If sending is not available, the app offers your e-mail app or the clipboard instead, and nothing is sent by the app itself. Feedback e-mails are kept as long as needed to handle them.

4. Advertising

The free version shows one banner advertisement at the bottom of the product list, served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To do this, the SDK may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and device and app information, under Google's advertising policies. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is loaded, and you can change your choice later from Remove ads in the app. Ad requests carry none of your product data. No other ad formats are used. Advertising stops entirely after the one-time "remove ads" purchase.

5. Purchases

The one-time "remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing under Google Play's terms and privacy policy. The app receives only the purchase state and stores it on the device so ads stay off while offline. Evervoya does not receive your payment details.

6. Notifications

Reminders (warranty ending, care due, claim follow-up) are computed and shown on the device by the app itself. They use the notification permission you grant and can be tuned or turned off in Settings or in Android settings.

7. Android device backup

Android may include this app's data in the device backup it makes to your Google account (Settings → System → Backup). That backup is run and controlled by Android and by you; the app does not manage it, read it or send anything to it. Turn it off in Android settings if you do not want it.

9. Third-party services

Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform, Google Play Billing, Sign in with Google / Credential Manager, Google ML Kit text recognition (on-device model; no cloud call), Google Apps Script (feedback relay). Each is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy. The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

10. Retention and deletion

Your records stay on your device until you delete them in the app (single products, bulk clean-up, or "Replace all" on import) or uninstall the app, which removes all app data. Sign out removes the stored Google identity. Backups you exported are yours to delete from wherever you saved them. Feedback e-mails can be deleted on request to the address below.

11. Children

ProductLog is a household tool for adults and is not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

12. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date and summarised in the app's release notes. Questions and requests: evervoya.studio@gmail.com.

The in-app Disclaimer & limitation of liability and the Terms of Service (Settings → Legal) apply together with this policy.

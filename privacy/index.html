Privacy

Evervoya Studio

Updated 3 November 2026

Evervoya Studio makes local-first Android apps. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked below, that describes feature by feature what stays on your phone, what leaves it and why. This page covers what our apps have in common, including how they use Google sign-in and Google APIs.

App privacy policies

  • Evervoya Optivo: SEO checks for your website, with optional Search Console and Google Analytics data
  • Evervoya ProductLog: receipts, warranties and claims for the things you own
  • Genivo: quiz duels
  • Asia Run

Google sign-in

Where an app offers "Sign in with Google", Google performs the sign-in. The app keeps your Google account identifier, name and e-mail address on your phone to show who is signed in, unless that app's policy says otherwise. Evervoya Studio runs no account server for Optivo or ProductLog.

Google API data

Some apps let you connect Google services to your own account: Evervoya Optivo reads your Google Search Console and Google Analytics data (read-only) and can keep an encrypted copy of its own workspace in a hidden app folder of your Google Drive. That data goes directly between your phone and Google. It is used only to show it to you and to provide the features you ask for, it is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with third parties and never sent to Evervoya Studio. You can disconnect at any time in the app or in your Google Account under Security, Third-party apps.

Our apps' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

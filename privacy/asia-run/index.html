Privacy policy

Asia Run

Effective 3 November 2026 · Applies to the Android game "Asia Run — Adventures of Valliant and Miko" (package com.asiarun.game) by Evervoya Studio

Asia Run is an endless-runner game. This policy describes what the game keeps on your phone, what leaves your phone and why, which third-party services are involved, and how you delete your online score.

1. Data that stays on your phone

Your progress (chapters, passport stamps, coins, badges, challenges, wardrobe), your settings, the device leaderboard with the name you choose, and which purchases you own are stored only on your phone. Android may include app data in the device backup it makes to your Google account; that backup is run and controlled by Android and by you.

2. Global leaderboard

When the phone is online, the game signs in anonymously to Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). No name, e-mail address or password is asked for. Firebase stores:

  • a random account identifier, kept on the phone so the same identity is used next time;
  • one leaderboard row under that identifier: the name you typed, your best score, chapters cleared, the place and country you reached, and the date.

That row is visible to other players. The game posts it only while the name field on the result screen is filled in; leave it empty and nothing is posted. Do not use your real name if you prefer not to be identified.

3. Advertising

The free version shows a banner on menu and result screens and offers an optional rewarded ad after a chapter, served by the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To do this, the SDK may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, and device and app information, under Google's advertising policies. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is loaded. Ads are never shown during a run. Advertising stops after the one-time "ad-free support" purchase.

4. Purchases

Two one-time purchases, the Miko running companion and ad-free support, are processed by Google Play Billing under Google Play's terms and privacy policy. The game receives only the purchase state and stores it on the phone; purchases are restored from your Google Play account. Evervoya does not receive your payment details.

5. Feedback you send

When you send feedback, the game sends your message and, only if you tick it, your device model and Android version, to Evervoya Studio through a relay. Feedback is kept as long as needed to handle it.

6. No analytics, no crash reporting

The game includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Only the services named in this policy receive data.

8. Third-party services

Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore), Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform, Google Play Billing, Google Apps Script (feedback relay). Each is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy.

9. Delete your online score and data

In the game: on any result screen, under Global leaderboard, press Delete my online score. This removes your row from the global leaderboard. Clearing the game's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling it, removes everything on the phone, including the anonymous identifier, so a later install starts as a new player.

Without the game: write to evervoya.studio@gmail.com with the subject "Asia Run data deletion", the name shown on the leaderboard and your best score so we can identify the row. We handle the request by hand and confirm by e-mail.

Purchases stay with your Google Play account. Feedback e-mails can be deleted on request.

10. Children

Asia Run is not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital consent where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

11. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date. Questions and requests: evervoya.studio@gmail.com.

The in-game Disclaimer, Terms of Use, Local Data and Intellectual Property pages (Settings) apply together with this policy.
